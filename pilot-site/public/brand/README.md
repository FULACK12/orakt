# ORAKT brand and geographic assets

- `orakt-icon-master.png`: original transparent production master, generated for ORAKT using the built-in image generator on 2026-09-25. Prompt: premium precision-sculpted metallic violet/cyan concentric orbit, inward folded chevron and upper-right node; preserve the existing ORAKT motif; no lettering or backdrop.
- WebP, favicon and touch icon are resized exports of the same master, not separately redrawn marks.
- `world-land-points.json`: sampled Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, public domain. Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson . Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Decorative overview, not a political-boundary or live-event dataset.
- World and Chains are original product demonstrations, inspired by Runpod's isometric network presentation and the layered animation direction of MotionSites. No third-party animations or videos are redistributed.
